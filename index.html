THE CLASSIC RULES

How to play 2048

01

One push, whole board

Choose up, down, left or right and every tile slides as far as it can in that direction. The board starts with two tiles.

02

Equal tiles fuse

Two tiles of the same value that run into each other merge into one tile of double the value, and that value is added to your score.

03

One merge per tile

A tile created by a merge cannot merge again in the same move, and the pair nearest the wall you pushed toward always merges first.

04

Every move costs a cell

A direction is only legal if something actually moves. After each legal move a new tile appears in a random empty cell — a 2 nine times out of ten, otherwise a 4.

05

Reach the target

Build the target tile to win — 2048 on the classic 4×4, and 256, 4096 or 8192 on the other grids. You can keep playing for a bigger score afterwards.

06

The board fills up

The game ends when the grid is full and no two orthogonal neighbours match. Keep your largest tile pinned in a corner and the rows sorted around it.
